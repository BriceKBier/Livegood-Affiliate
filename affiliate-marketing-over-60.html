Affiliate Marketing Over 60: A Realistic Beginner's Guide

Affiliate marketing is simply earning a commission or referral reward when someone uses your tracked link and completes the required action. The concept is simple. Building enough trust and traffic to make it worthwhile is the harder part.

How it works

You recommend a product or service through a special tracked link. If a visitor uses that link and completes a qualifying purchase or signup, the company may pay you a commission or referral reward.

You do not need to be a tech expert

A simple website, social account, email audience or video channel can be enough to begin. The more important skill is explaining something clearly and helping people make a decision.

Traffic comes before commissions

A referral link nobody sees is unlikely to earn anything. Useful articles that answer real questions can attract search visitors over time. That is why helpful content and internal linking matter more than filling a page with advertisements.

Disclose the relationship

If you may receive compensation from a link, say so clearly. Transparency is good for visitors and should be part of the site from the beginning.

What to avoid

  • Programs promising guaranteed income
  • Large upfront purchases just to participate
  • Promoting products you know nothing about
  • Copying manufacturer claims without checking them
  • Expecting immediate search traffic

A sensible first goal

Choose a topic you genuinely know, create a small group of useful pages around it, and recommend only products or services that naturally fit those pages. Build the audience first.
