Best Vitamins for Adults Over 60 (2026)

A sensible starting point for understanding commonly discussed nutrients and better questions to ask before buying supplements.

Important: Supplements are personal. Review labels and discuss them with your physician or pharmacist, especially if you take prescription medication.

What to look for

Pay attention to the actual dose, ingredient form, cost and whether the product addresses a real need. More is not automatically better.

Common topics

Vitamin D, calcium, magnesium, B vitamins and other nutrients are frequently discussed for older adults, but individual needs vary.

What matters more than a supplement

Regular activity, a balanced diet, sleep and appropriate medical care do more for long-term health than any single bottle.
