Testing and product information

One feature I like to look for with supplements is transparency. LiveGood says its products are independently tested for purity, potency and safety and publishes testing information on its website. That gives shoppers something more concrete to review than advertising language alone.

What should you realistically expect from collagen?

Collagen supplements are not a magic fix. Research on collagen varies by formulation, dose and outcome, and results can differ from person to person. Be cautious with promises of dramatic weight loss, wrinkle removal, pain treatment or rapid changes. A supplement should complement—not replace—a balanced diet or medical care.

Who should check with a healthcare professional first?

If you take prescription medicines, have a medical condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have allergies or dietary restrictions, review the full current label and discuss supplements with an appropriate healthcare professional before starting them.
