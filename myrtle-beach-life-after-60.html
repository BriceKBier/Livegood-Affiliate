Myrtle Beach Life After 60

A practical local perspective on coastal living, community, everyday tradeoffs and the questions worth asking before a move.

The good

Coastal living can offer an active lifestyle, abundant recreation and a wide range of communities. But the right fit depends heavily on traffic, neighborhood, insurance, healthcare access and your preferred pace of life.

The tradeoffs

Tourist-season traffic, storm exposure, insurance costs and changing development patterns are worth researching carefully.
