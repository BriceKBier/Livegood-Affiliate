20 Free Brain Games for Adults & Seniors

Give your brain a fun workout with logic puzzles, riddles, number patterns, word challenges and quick memory warm-ups. Reveal each answer only when you are ready.

Brain games you can do anytime

You do not need an app, account or download for these free brain games. Work through them alone, compare answers with a friend, or use a few as a quick coffee-break challenge. The collection mixes easy and tricky questions so you can jump around instead of doing them in order.

Just for fun: These puzzles are recreational. They are not a medical test and are not intended to diagnose, prevent or treat memory or cognitive conditions.

20 free puzzles, riddles and thinking games

1

Number pattern

What number comes next? 3, 6, 11, 18, 27, ?

Reveal answer

38. The differences are +3, +5, +7, +9, then +11.

2

Classic riddle

What has a face and two hands but no arms or legs?

Reveal answer

A clock.

3

Word logic

Which word is the odd one out: inch, ounce, yard, foot?

Reveal answer

Ounce. The others are units of length.

4

Quick math

A farmer has 15 sheep. All but 6 wander away. How many remain?

Reveal answer

6. “All but 6” means 6 stayed.

5

Letter pattern

What comes next? B, E, I, N, ?

Reveal answer

T. Move forward +3, +4, +5, then +6 letters.

6

Family logic

Two fathers and two sons go fishing. They catch three fish and each person gets one fish. How?

Reveal answer

There are only three people: a grandfather, his son, and his grandson.

7

Missing number

What number replaces the question mark? 2, 6, 18, 54, ?

Reveal answer

162. Multiply by 3 each time.

8

Word scramble

Unscramble these letters into something you use to think: N I B R A.

Reveal answer

BRAIN.

9

Trick question

How many months have 28 days?

Reveal answer

All 12. Every month has at least 28 days.

10

Clock challenge

At exactly 6:00, what is the angle between the hour and minute hands?

Reveal answer

180 degrees.

11

Sequence

What number comes next? 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, ?

Reveal answer

13. Each number is the sum of the previous two.

12

Riddle

The more you take, the more you leave behind. What are they?

Reveal answer

Footsteps.

13

Which does not belong?

8, 27, 64, 100, 125

Reveal answer

100. The others are perfect cubes: 2³, 3³, 4³ and 5³.

14

Price puzzle

A bat and ball cost $1.10 total. The bat costs $1 more than the ball. What does the ball cost?

Reveal answer

5 cents. The bat is $1.05, totaling $1.10.

15

Word connection

What one word can follow HEAD and come before HOUSE?

Reveal answer

LIGHT: headlight and lighthouse.

16

Day logic

If yesterday was two days before Friday, what day is today?

Reveal answer

Thursday. Two days before Friday is Wednesday, so today is Thursday.

17

Number relationship

If 3 → 9 and 4 → 16, then 7 → ?

Reveal answer

49. Square the first number.

18

Handshakes

Four people each shake hands with every other person exactly once. How many handshakes occur?

Reveal answer

6. The unique pairs are AB, AC, AD, BC, BD and CD.

19

Memory warm-up

Read these once, look away, then try to recall all six: river, lamp, apple, train, blue, key.

Reveal tip

Try making one silly picture that connects the words—for example, a blue train crossing a river while carrying an apple, lamp and key.

20

Final riddle

I am full of holes, but I can still hold water. What am I?

Reveal answer

A sponge.
