Is LiveGood Worth It?

A balanced look at the membership, products, pricing and optional business opportunity.

The honest answer is: it depends on what you want. Some people are interested in member pricing on wellness products. Others are interested in the optional affiliate opportunity.

Potential advantages

  • Member pricing on wellness products.
  • No inventory requirement for affiliates.
  • Online ordering and direct shipping.
  • The business side is optional.

Potential drawbacks

  • Income is never guaranteed.
  • Network marketing does not suit everyone.
  • Building customers or a team takes time.
  • Products still need to be evaluated individually.
My bottom line: Treat it as a real membership and a real business—not a promise of overnight success.
