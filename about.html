About the founder

Hi, I’m Brice Bier.

I created UCanWorkFromHome to help adults 50+ make clearer decisions about health, flexible income and the next chapter of life.

“My goal is to help readers make smarter decisions about their health, time and money—without pressure.”

Why I created this site

After years in business and real estate, I became interested in the possibilities of building income online. I liked the flexibility, the low overhead and the fact that a person could keep learning and building at any age.

What I write about

UCanWorkFromHome covers work-from-home opportunities, retirement-friendly income ideas, side hustles, affiliate marketing, healthy aging and wellness topics.

My editorial promise

I will explain what I can, acknowledge what I cannot know, and avoid presenting an opportunity as guaranteed income. Affiliate relationships may exist, but they do not change the responsibility to explain drawbacks.
