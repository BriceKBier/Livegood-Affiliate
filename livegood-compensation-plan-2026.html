LiveGood compensation plan 2026: how it works

Searching for the LiveGood compensation plan or a LiveGood compensation plan PDF? Here is a straightforward, plain-English overview of the seven earning methods, qualifications to watch, and where to find the current official plan.

Looking for the LiveGood compensation plan PDF? The safest source is LiveGood's current official compensation-plan page. Compensation terms can change, so an older PDF saved or reposted elsewhere may no longer match the current plan. View the current official LiveGood compensation plan →

The basic idea

Membership and the affiliate opportunity are separate choices. Someone may join only for member product pricing. A person choosing the optional affiliate side may qualify for commissions under LiveGood's current rules, ranks and policies.

The seven earning methods LiveGood describes

1. Weekly Fast Start Commissions

These are tied to personally referred people who join as members or affiliates. The official plan currently explains a possible combined $25 first-week commission when someone joins as both, subject to its terms.

2. Matrix Commissions

Qualified affiliates receive a position in LiveGood's 2-by-15 matrix. Monthly commissions are based on active membership volume within eligible levels; rank determines how many levels may pay.

3. Matching Bonuses

At qualifying ranks, an affiliate may earn a percentage match on eligible matrix commissions earned by people in the organization.

4. Retail Commissions

When a referred retail customer purchases at retail price, the plan currently pays from the difference between retail and member pricing, following its rank structure.

5. Customer Acquisition Bonuses

LiveGood describes a one-time bonus connected with a personally enrolled member's first qualifying product order, subject to order and household limits.

6. Influencer Bonuses

Additional bonuses may be available for affiliates who meet LiveGood's current personal-enrollment and qualification requirements.

7. Diamond and Crown Diamond Pools

Top qualifying ranks may share in designated company sales pools as described in the official plan.

What should you check before joining?

  • The current affiliate fee and membership cost
  • Rank and active-status requirements
  • Which earnings are weekly and which are monthly
  • Whether you are comfortable consistently finding and helping customers
  • LiveGood's current policies and compensation terms
Important: This is an independent summary, not an official LiveGood document or an income guarantee. Rules can change. Earnings depend on effort, skill, customers, team activity and qualification. Many participants may earn little or nothing. Verify details on the official plan.

LiveGood compensation plan FAQ

Is there a LiveGood compensation plan PDF?

People often search for a PDF copy, but the better reference is LiveGood's current official compensation-plan page because it can reflect changes more reliably than an older downloaded or reposted PDF.

How many ways does the LiveGood compensation plan describe earning?

The current plan describes seven earning methods. They include fast-start, matrix, matching, retail and customer-acquisition commissions, plus influencer bonuses and designated pools for qualifying top ranks.

Do you have to become an affiliate to buy LiveGood products?

No. Product membership and participation in the affiliate opportunity are separate choices. Someone can choose member pricing without pursuing the business opportunity.

Does joining guarantee income?

No. This page does not make an income guarantee. Earnings depend on qualifications, customers, activity, skill and other factors, and some participants may earn little or nothing.
