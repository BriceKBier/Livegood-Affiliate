UCanWorkFromHome.com — Version 13.8.0

WHAT CHANGED
- Doubled the regular Logic Challenge content from three to six activities in
  Kindergarten and Grades 1–3.
- Added a separate advanced Trophy Round to every grade.
- Completing six regular challenges fills six stars and unlocks the Trophy Round.
- Each Trophy Round contains three harder, grade-appropriate activities.
- Completing all three advanced challenges awards an animated gold trophy.
- The earned trophy is saved in the browser and remains displayed on that computer.
- Total logic content is now 36 activities: 24 regular and 12 advanced.
- Existing counting boxes and all sixteen narrated math stories remain intact.

FILES CHANGED
- math-k3.html
- math-voice-lessons.js
- style.css

VERSION 13.8.1 MUSIC & TROPHY CELEBRATION UPDATE

Homepage:
- Added Brice's Big Band Bounce, an original swing-style instrumental.
- Music begins only when the visitor presses Play.
- Includes Pause and volume controls.
- Music pauses when the browser tab is hidden.

Kids Math Adventures:
- Added an original children's celebration song.
- Added animated fireworks and a three-trophy Super Learner celebration.
- The special celebration runs automatically when three different grade-level
  gold trophies have been earned on the same device.
VERSION 13.8.2 TROPHY REWARD UPDATE

- Every completed grade-level Trophy Round now launches music and fireworks.
- The reward names the level and displays its gold trophy.
- Earning the third grade-level trophy still launches the larger three-trophy
  Super Learner celebration.
VERSION 13.8.3 YOUNG LEARNER DIALOGUE UPDATE

- Rewrote the entire Kindergarten experience for children around ages four
  and five: directions, story questions, pattern games, rules, hints, praise,
  buttons, progress messages and trophy celebrations.
- Uses shorter, happier phrases such as "Wow! You did it!", "shiny gold
  trophy," "superstar" and "give yourself a big high five."
- Renamed formal Kindergarten terms such as "logic rule," "advanced
  challenge" and "Trophy Round" to friendly phrases such as "picture game,"
  "listen to this" and "Special Trophy Game."
VERSION 13.8.4 VOICE POLISH

- Replaced every Kindergarten use of "Yay" with "Very good."
- Expanded preference for natural/neural Windows voices.
- Changed speech from an unusually slow pace to a smoother conversational
  pace, with a slightly warmer pitch and gentler volume.
VERSION 13.9.0 PERSONALIZED TROPHY SHELF

- Added a permanent trophy shelf backdrop to Math Adventures.
- The first earned trophy asks the child for a name with grown-up help.
- Every saved grade-level trophy appears on the shelf with the child's name
  and grade engraved on its plaque.
- The name and trophies are stored only in that browser on that device.
- Added a Change player name button for siblings or another learner.
- Trophy celebrations now greet the child by name.
VERSION 13.9.1 MULTI-CHILD TROPHY SHELF CORRECTION

- Removed the confusing Change player name button.
- Every time a child enters a different grade, the site asks, "Who is playing
  this level?"
- Each grade trophy stores its own winner name, so Kindergarten, Grade 1,
  Grade 2 and Grade 3 trophies may all belong to different children.
- The shared shelf displays the correct engraved name on each separate trophy.
- Entering a name for a previously earned trophy updates that trophy's plaque.
- Added a compact shelf that remains visible throughout Math Adventures, with
  separate rows labeled K, 1, 2 and 3.
- The compact shelf immediately displays each earned trophy and engraved name;
  it disappears when the visitor exits Kids' World.
VERSION 13.9.2 TROPHY ANNOUNCER

- Trophy victories now use a separate, strong male announcer voice.
- The announcer congratulates the child by name and identifies the grade trophy.
- The announcement plays clearly before the victory music begins.
VERSION 13.9.3 VISIT-ONLY PLAYER AND TROPHIES

- The player-name box is blank whenever a level is entered.

VERSION 13.9.4 GRADE-SELECTION FLOW FIX

- Removed the automatic name prompt that appeared when Math Adventures opened.

VERSION 13.9.5 STARTING-SEQUENCE CORRECTION

- The large "Choose a grade" button now asks for the child's name first.
