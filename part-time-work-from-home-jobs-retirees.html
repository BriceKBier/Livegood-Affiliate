Part-Time Work From Home Jobs for Retirees

Retirement does not always mean you want to stop working completely. A part-time remote job can provide extra income, structure and social contact without returning to a full-time commute.

Good part-time remote categories

  • Customer service
  • Virtual receptionist or scheduler
  • Bookkeeping
  • Online tutoring
  • Remote sales or lead follow-up
  • Administrative support
  • Freelance writing, editing or consulting

Use the experience you already have

Years of work experience can be an advantage. Someone with a background in accounting, education, real estate, insurance, healthcare administration, sales or technical work may be able to earn more by using that knowledge rather than starting with an entry-level gig.

How many hours make sense?

Think about the lifestyle first. Ten to twenty hours a week may be enough for supplemental income without turning retirement into another full-time job. Pay attention to fixed schedules: some 'part-time' remote jobs still require specific shifts.

Employee job or independent contractor?

Employee positions may offer steadier schedules and payroll withholding. Contractor work can offer more flexibility, but you generally handle your own taxes and expenses. Know which arrangement you are accepting before you start.

Avoid the retirement-job scam trap

Older adults are frequently targeted with easy-money offers. Never assume 'work from home' means legitimate. Verify the employer and be particularly suspicious of upfront fees, fake checks and requests to move money.
